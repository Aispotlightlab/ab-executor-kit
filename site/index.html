Alberta Executor Kit

For the family member who was named executor of an Alberta estate and has never done this before. One purchase per estate. No monthly plan, nothing to cancel, nothing stored on our servers — you download four spreadsheets and keep them.

  • Dated task checklist — 19 tasks from the date of death, in the order Alberta's process expects them (death certificate, will, securing property, notices, Surrogate application, final tax return, clearance certificate, distribution), each with a target date computed from your date of death.
  • Expense & reimbursement ledger — every dollar you pay out of pocket, so the estate pays you back.
  • Date-of-death asset inventory and beneficiary distribution sheet.
